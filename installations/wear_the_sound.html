Wear the Sound

Exhibited at the Mies Van Der Rohe Pavilion in the 2025 Sónar +D and at the BCN Games Fest.

This installation responds to the need of attending music festivals without crowds, with moderate music volume, accessible to people with reduced mobility, and supported by a team dedicated to facilitating the experience for all visitors. It's a proposal that becomes an experimental space open to feel the music with your body.

A series of haptic and sensory interfaces take the form of reclining supports or wearables that can be worn and shared with others, allowing music to be experienced through the skin.

We collaborated with music artist Xavi Manzanares (@xamanza) who created a music console that connects to the wearable via OSC. Through the console, people can modify the music, and the wearable's vibrations shift simultaneously, creating a unique, immersive experience.

This project is a prototype of a Sónar space designed for a wider audience that aims to leave no one out.

This experience took place at the Mies Van Der Rohe Pavilion, an icon of design. We took the architecture of the space into account when designing the wearables.
