caleidoscopio

r reset
s save
espacio play/pause
d diameter
a angle
f filling (line or filled)
click anywhere to draw a flower

Originally built in Processing and translated to p5.js with the help of Claude AI to share on the web.

I like the Processing version more, I was able to play with transparent spheres creating interesting effects. The way spheres are handled in p5.js and the fact that they need light to be setup made it harder to create a color mixing effect.
